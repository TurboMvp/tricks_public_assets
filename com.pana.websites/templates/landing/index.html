What it does

01

Reads the diff, not just the title

Commit messages lie. Patchnote looks at what changed and writes what it means for a user.

02

Writes in your voice

Feed it three old changelogs. New ones sound like you on a good day.

03

Ships everywhere at once

One click posts to your docs, an in app widget, Slack and an email digest.

04

Hides the boring parts

Dependency bumps and refactors stay out unless a user would notice.

05

Groups by what users care about

Added, fixed, faster. Not by repository or by who merged it.

06

Keeps a human in charge

Nothing goes out until someone on your team presses publish.

// why

Commit messages lie. Diffs don't. Patchnote reads every changed line, so your users read only what matters to them.

Three commands

  1. $ patchnote connect

    Link a repository. Read only, takes a minute.

  2. $ patchnote draft

    Get a draft for everything merged since your last release.

  3. $ patchnote publish

    Review, tweak a line, and send it everywhere at once.
