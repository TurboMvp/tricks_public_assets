FAQ

Questions

Still curious? Write to us from inside the app.

Is Drift a medical device?

No. It helps with habits, not disorders. If you think you have a sleep condition, talk to a doctor.

Do I need a watch?

No. The phone on your nightstand is enough. A watch adds heart rate.

Where is my data?

On your phone. Encrypted backup is optional and off by default.

Can I cancel Plus?

Any time from your store account, and you keep Plus until the period ends.
