Before you write

A partner answers every enquiry within two working days.

What does a project cost?

Most identities land between €25k and €60k, products from €60k. We quote a fixed fee after a first call.

Do you work with in-house teams?

Often. We pair with your designers and engineers and hand over everything, source files included.

Where are you based?

Rotterdam. About half our clients are elsewhere in Europe and we travel for workshops.

When can you start?

We take on four projects at a time. The next opening is usually six to eight weeks out.

Have something worth keeping?

Tell us what you are making. You will hear back from a partner, not a form.
