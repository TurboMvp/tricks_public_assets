01Dusk

You arrive as the light goes.

No reception desk, no check-in screen. A key on a hook, a fire already laid, and a view that does the talking for the first evening.

02Forest

Mornings belong to the trees.

A forty minute loop through old pine, a sauna at the end of it, and nowhere you need to be until dinner.

03Sea

Nights belong to the water.

Eleven cabins face the fjord. On clear nights the bay holds the whole sky, and on cloudy ones the sound of it is enough.

Practical things

Anything else, write and Marit replies the same day.

How do I get there?

Fly to Bodø, then a two hour drive. The last three kilometres are a footpath, and we carry your bags.

Is there wifi?

In the main house only. The cabins are deliberately offline.

When is the best time?

June for light that never ends, February for snow and the northern lights.

Can I cancel?

Free cancellation up to thirty days before arrival.

Come and stay a while

Three nights minimum. All meals cooked with what is in season, from €320 a night.
