Before you buy

Anything else, write to us. A person replies within a day.

Does it work on induction?

Yes. A steel disc is fired into the base, so it works on induction, gas and electric hobs.

Is every kettle the same?

Each is thrown by hand, so the glaze varies slightly. That is part of the point.

How do I clean it?

Rinse with warm water and let it air dry. Oil the handle once a season.

What if it breaks?

Chips and cracks are repaired free for ten years. Send a photo and we arrange the rest.
