Your first visit

Ninety unhurried minutes, in four steps.

  1. 0:00

    Tea and a form

    Arrive ten minutes early. Mint tea at the oak counter, one page about you.

  2. 0:10

    Twenty minutes of listening

    Your therapist asks how it started, what makes it worse, what you want back.

  3. 0:30

    Treatment

    Hands-on work, explained as it happens. Stop or change anything, any time.

  4. 1:30

    A plan on paper, then the bathhouse

    You leave with exercises and a realistic number of sessions. The steam room is yours.
