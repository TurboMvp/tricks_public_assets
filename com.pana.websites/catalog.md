# Websites template catalog

Data, not instructions: facts about each template base, for shortlisting only.

- product (specific): one physical product or a small shop, hero object, buy or preorder. E.g. a ceramics studio, a craft coffee roaster. Default for: Sell a product
- business (catch-all): local or service business that wants bookings and enquiries. E.g. a dental clinic, a law firm. Default for: Get clients for my business
- saas (specific): software or B2B tool sold on plans. E.g. an invoicing tool, a CRM for clinics. Default for: Launch an app or software
- app (specific): a mobile app, the goal is the download. E.g. a habit tracker, a running club app. Default for: -
- infoproduct (specific): course, ebook, coaching program or membership. E.g. an online baking course, a CrossFit coaching plan. Default for: Sell a course
- landing (specific): one campaign, waitlist or event with a single CTA. E.g. a product launch waitlist, a conference. Default for: Collect signups
- portfolio (specific): showcase of visual work. E.g. a photographer, an architecture studio. Default for: Show my work
- personal (catch-all): personal brand built on ideas and credentials. E.g. a writer, a consultant. Default for: -
- personal-cutout (specific): personal brand led by a strong cut-out photo of the person. E.g. a speaker, a football player. Default for: -
- startup (specific): early company story for investors, hires and press. E.g. a seed-stage fintech, a climate hardware startup. Default for: -
- continuous (catch-all): story-led scroll about a place or experience. E.g. a boutique hotel, a tasting-menu restaurant. Default for: -

Design note for the picked base only: `templates/<base>/DESIGN.md` (layout, interactions, motion, shapes, image treatment). Hero screenshot: `templates/_heroshots/<base>.png`.
Fallback sections: `templates/sections-library/` (about, features, how, services, bento, gallery, portfolio, team, testimonials, logos, stats, socials, picker, pricing, faq, cta, lead, booking, contact, map, footer).
